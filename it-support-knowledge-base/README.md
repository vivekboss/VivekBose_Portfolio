## 🧭 My Troubleshooting Approach

1. **Listen & clarify** – What happened? When did it start? What changed?
2. **Reproduce & scope** – One user or many? One device or all?
3. **Start simple** – Cables, power, restart, basic settings
4. **Isolate** – Hardware, software, network or user error?
5. **Fix & verify** – Confirm with the user that it's resolved
6. **Document** – Log symptoms, cause, fix and time taken
7. **Escalate when needed** – With clear notes so nobody repeats work

## ⚠️ Note

These guides are general best-practice references for learning and demonstration. Always follow your organisation's IT policies and escalate to the proper team for anything beyond your access level.
